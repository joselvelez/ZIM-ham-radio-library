OVERVIEW

The BuddiHEX is a 6-band high performance hexbeam. Specifically designed to be light in weight this antenna is a great choice for both portable and base station use. The antenna packs into a set of compact components for quick setup.

INSTALLING ANTENNA WIRES

The BuddiHEX is a six-band antenna comprised of six nested antennas driven from a single feed-point located at the top of the vertical feed-point assembly. Each of these separate antennas consists of a two-element beam using a driven element and a parasitic reflector arranged to form a modified Moxon beam antenna (Figure 9).

MOUNTING THE ANTENNA

Setup is best accomplished as a two-man effort because care must be taking particularly with tensioning the guy lines on a telescopic mast.

The lower hub of the BuddiHEX has a built-in adapter for use with the MastWerks series of masts. The MastWerks mast has a non-circular profile so if you intend to use the antenna with a circular mast the use of an adapter will be required.

If the top of the mast is fitted with a spring button remove the button before installing the BuddiHEX (Keep the button in a safe place in case you need it for some other antenna type)

  1. Setup and level the tripod

  2. Insert the un-extended mast into the tripod and attach the guy rings.

  3. Fully open the collar lock on the bottom of the BuddiHEX lower hub

ANTENNA DISASSEMBLY

The antenna should be disassembled in the reverse

procedure to assembly.

  1. Remove the antenna element wires starting with the innermost (6m) wires and moving towards the center.

  2. After removing each element wrap the wire as instructed in the next section ready for storage.

  3. Disconnect the peripheral cord at the end of each of the spreaders (leave cord attached to the end of the spreader).

  4. Carefully release the end of the support cords from the central hub to the end of each spreader (leave the cord attached to the end of the spreader). After disconnecting the first support cord remove the cord from the spreader diametrically opposite to keep the tension on the vertical support assembly as symmetrical as possible.

  5. Carefully lift the complete antenna assembly and fully secure into the top of the mast. Lock the antenna in place by closing the BuddiHEX adapter collar lock.

  6. Attach the feedline to the feed-point and use a Velcro strap to the mast to take the weight of the feedline.

  7. Attach guy lines to the upper and lower guy rings and place ground anchors around the tripod to secure the bottom end of the lines.

  8. Push up the mast slowly beginning with Section B (do not fully extend Section A).

  9. When the mast is fully extended (except Section A) and the guy lines are properly secured the antenna is ready for use.

All these parts can be stowed in the soft carry case. Careful packing of the antenna will be well rewarded when you come to deploy it next time. Please follow these instructions carefully.

Wire winders

The wire assemblies are stored on three different sizes of wire winder. The 20m and 17m are stored on the largest winder, 15m and 12m stored on the medium sized winder while 10m and 6m are stored on the smallest winder.

Each of the wire assemblies should be wound onto the specific wire winder using a “figure of eight” winding technique. This avoids putting twists and kinks into the wire.
