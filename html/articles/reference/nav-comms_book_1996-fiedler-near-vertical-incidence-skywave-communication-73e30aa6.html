‘The function of an antenna depends on whether it is transmitting or
receiving. A transmitting antenna transforms the output RF energy
produced by a radio transmitter (RFoutput power)into an electromagnetic
field that is radiated through space. In other words, the transmitting
antenna converts energy from one form to another form. The receiving
antenna reverses this process.
It transforms the electromagnetic field into
RF energy which is delivered toa radio receiver.

radio circuit, transmission is required between a transmitter

increase the energy pickuporgain in the favored direction, and reduce the
reception

requirements for transmitting and receiving antennas
