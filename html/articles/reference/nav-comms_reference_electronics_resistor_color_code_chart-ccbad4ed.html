4-Band • 5-Band • 6-Band Reference

The electronic color code is used to identify the values and tolerances of resistors. They generally consist of multiple bands on a resistor. There is a 4, 5 and 6 band color code for resistors. The 4 and 5 band color codes are generally the ones you will see the most. The 6 band color code is much rarer. The 5 band color code has one more digit than the 4 band color code and is used for more precise values. The 6 band color code has an additional band for the temperature coefficient, which is why it is less common. There are some exceptions in the resistor color code. For example, a 0 Ohm resistor only has one black band.

4-Band Resistor Color Code

To read a 4-band resistor, the first two bands are digits, the third is the multiplier, and the fourth is the tolerance.

Color 1st Band 2nd Band Multiplier Tolerance
Black 0 0 ×1 —
Brown 1 1 ×10 ±1%
Red 2 2 ×100 ±2%
Orange 3 3 ×1k —
Yellow 4 4 ×10k —
Green 5 5 ×100k ±0.5%
Blue 6 6 ×1M ±0.25%
Violet 7 7 ×10M ±0.10%
Grey 8 8 ×100M ±0.05%
White 9 9 ×1G —
Gold — — ×0.1 ±5%
Silver — — ×0.01 ±10%

Example (from HyperElectronic): 470 Ω ±10%

Yellow (4) – Violet (7) – Brown (×10) – Silver (±10%). Calculation: 47 × 10 = 470 Ω.

5-Band Resistor Color Code

A 5-band resistor provides three significant digits before the multiplier for higher precision.

Color 1st 2nd 3rd Multiplier Tolerance
Black 0 0 0 ×1 —
Brown 1 1 1 ×10 ±1%
Red 2 2 2 ×100 ±2%
Orange 3 3 3 ×1k —
Yellow 4 4 4 ×10k —
Green 5 5 5 ×100k ±0.5%
Blue 6 6 6 ×1M ±0.25%
Violet 7 7 7 ×10M ±0.10%
Grey 8 8 8 ×100M ±0.05%
White 9 9 9 ×1G —
Gold — — — ×0.1 ±5%
Silver — — — ×0.01 ±10%

Example (from HyperElectronic): 499 Ω ±1%

Yellow (4) – White (9) – White (9) – Black (×1) – Brown (±1%). Calculation: 499 × 1 = 499 Ω.

6-Band Resistor Color Code + Temperature Coefficient

The 6th band indicates the temperature coefficient in ppm/K (how much the resistance changes with temperature). This is mainly used on precision metal-film resistors.

Color Digit Multiplier Tolerance Temp. Coeff.
Black 0 ×1 — 250 ppm/K
Red Orange 2 3 ×100 ×1k ±2% — 50 ppm/K 15 ppm/K
Yellow 4 ×10k — 25 ppm/K
Green 5 ×100k ±0.5% 20 ppm/K
Blue 6 ×1M ±0.25% 10 ppm/K
Violet 7 ×10M ±0.10% 5 ppm/K
Grey 8 ×100M ±0.05% 1 ppm/K
White 9 ×1G — —
Gold — ×0.1 ±5% —
Silver — ×0.01 ±10% —

Notes: Gold (×0.1) and silver (×0.01) multipliers are used for values under 10 Ω. No tolerance band means ±20%. Always verify important values with a multimeter — lighting, aging, and manufacturing variations can affect color appearance.
